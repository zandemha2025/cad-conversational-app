## Your Task

Analyze the part geometry and generate appropriate GD&T callouts following ASME Y14.5-2018. Consider the part's function, mating surfaces, and manufacturing process.

For each callout, determine:
1. The geometric characteristic symbol (position, flatness, perpendicularity, etc.)
2. The tolerance value with appropriate units
3. Datum references (if applicable) in priority order (A, B, C)
4. Which feature the callout applies to
5. A brief rationale explaining why this callout is needed

## Rules

- Primary datum (A) should be the largest planar face or the primary locating surface
- Secondary datum (B) should constrain the next degree of freedom (perpendicular to A)
- Tertiary datum (C) constrains the final rotational degree of freedom
- Bolt hole patterns MUST have position tolerance with MMC modifier where appropriate
- Datum surfaces MUST have flatness callouts
- Bored holes perpendicular to a datum face MUST have perpendicularity callouts
- Include surface finish (Ra) recommendations for critical surfaces
- Use realistic manufacturing tolerances (not overly tight):
  - General machined surfaces: flatness 0.05-0.10 mm
  - Bolt hole position: diameter 0.15-0.30 mm
  - Bore perpendicularity: 0.05-0.10 mm
  - Surface finish: Ra 0.8-3.2 um depending on function
